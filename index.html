About Name the Baby

Name the Baby is a one-page discovery and creation tool designed to make choosing a baby name feel less like searching a database and more like finding or building a name together.

The generator uses your selected filters plus your saves and passes during the current visit. V9 contains 1,500 name records, including 209 richly curated editorial profiles, plus official SSA 2020–2025 Top 200 popularity metadata for matching names. Saved favorites and local Name Battle scores are stored in your browser so they can remain available on the same device.

Privacy preview

Name the Baby keeps personal naming preferences local unless a visitor intentionally creates a shared ballot or submits a sponsor inquiry.

Favorites and local Name Battle scores stay in the browser. When the launch backend is connected, the site may record privacy-light product events such as page views, generations, saves, passes, Lab use, ballot creation, voting, and aggregate partner-offer impressions/clicks without sending surnames or exact generated-name choices in analytics. Shared ballots store the family/label text and ballot names entered by the creator. Sponsor inquiries store the contact information the sponsor submits.
